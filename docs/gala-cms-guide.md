# Gala Resort — Content Dashboard Guide

## Logging In

Go to: **https://galaresort.com/studio**

Click **Log in** and sign in with your Google account (galaresortllc@gmail.com). You will land directly in the content dashboard. Bookmark this link for easy access.

---

## What You Can Manage

The left sidebar has five sections:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Events** | Add, edit, and remove events from the calendar |
| **Cabins** | Update cabin descriptions, rates, and amenities |
| **Announcements** | Show or hide a banner at the top of the site |
| **Bar Info** | Update bar hours and food notes |
| **Gallery Images** | Add and organize photos by category |

---

## Managing Events

This is where you will spend most of your time.

### Adding a New Event
1. Click **Events** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Upcoming Events**
3. Click the **pencil icon** or **+** button in the top left
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Chris Hodgson — Live on the Patio"
   - **Start Date** — the date of the event
   - **End Date** — only needed for multi-day events (e.g. Memorial Day Weekend). Leave blank for single-day events
   - **Time** — e.g. "3:00 PM – 6:00 PM"
   - **Category** — choose from Live Music, Seasonal, Community, or Special
   - **Description** — a short description shown on the calendar
   - **Featured** — toggle on to highlight this event on the homepage
   - **Link** — optional. Paste in an artist website, Spotify page, or Facebook event link. Shows as a "More info" button on the site
   - **Image** — optional. Best size: 1200x675 px, JPG, under 500 KB
5. Click **Publish** in the bottom right

### Editing an Existing Event
1. Click **Events**, then **Upcoming Events**
2. Click the event you want to edit
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Publish**

### What Happens to Past Events
Events automatically stop showing on the site the day after they end — you do not need to delete them. They move to the **Past Events** folder in the sidebar where they stay accessible. If you want to reuse a past event (same artist, new date), open it from Past Events, update the date, and publish. It will automatically move back into Upcoming.

## Announcements

Use this to show a temporary banner at the top of every page — useful for opening day notices, weather closures, or special promotions.

1. Click **Announcements**
2. Click the existing announcement or create a new one
3. Write your message in the **Text** field
4. Toggle **Active** on to show it, or off to hide it
5. Click **Publish**

Only one announcement shows on the site at a time. If you have multiple announcements marked Active, the site will pick one unpredictably — so make sure only one is Active at any given time. Toggle the others off before turning a new one on.

---

## Cabins

Use this to update cabin descriptions, rates, guest counts, and amenities.

1. Click **Cabins** in the left sidebar
2. Click the cabin you want to edit
3. Update any fields as needed:
   - **Name** — the cabin name shown on the site
   - **Max Guests** — the maximum number of guests
   - **Nightly Rate / Weekly Rate** — shown on the cabin detail page
   - **Min Nights** — minimum booking length
   - **Dog Friendly** — toggle on or off
   - **Short Description** — the one-liner shown on the cabins listing page
   - **Description** — the full description shown on the cabin detail page
   - **Amenities** — list of amenities shown on the detail page
   - **Images** — upload cabin photos (1200px wide minimum, JPG, under 1 MB each)
4. Click **Publish**

Note: cabin changes go live within about 60 seconds.

## Gallery Images

Use this to add, update, or organize photos in the gallery.

### Adding a New Photo
1. Click **Gallery Images** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **+** button or pencil icon in the top left
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Image** — upload your photo (1200px wide minimum, JPG, under 1 MB)
   - **Alt Text** — describe the photo, e.g. "Guests enjoying the patio at sunset" (required for accessibility)
   - **Category** — choose from Waterfront, Cabins, Bar & Events, Grounds, or Life at The Gala
   - **Caption** — optional short caption shown in the lightbox
4. Click **Publish**

### Categories
- **Waterfront** — river, docks, aerial shots of the water
- **Cabins** — exterior and interior cabin photos
- **Bar & Events** — bar, patio, live music shots
- **Grounds** — property, campground, roads, amenities
- **Life at The Gala** — candid guest photos, people having fun, bar nights, events in action

---

## Bar Info

Use this to update your bar hours and food note shown on the Bar & Events page.

1. Click **Bar Info** in the left sidebar
2. Click the existing Bar Info document
3. Update the fields:
   - **Hours** — e.g. "Tuesday through Sunday, 11 AM to close"
   - **Closed Days** — e.g. "Closed Mondays"
   - **Food Note** — e.g. "Fresh pizza and bar favorites"
4. Click **Publish**

---

## A Few Things to Know

- **Changes go live within about 60 seconds** after you hit Publish
- **Draft vs. Published** — always click the **Draft** tab at the top of a document to make edits. The Published tab is read-only
- **Nothing is permanent** — you can always unpublish, edit, or delete anything you have added
- **Nothing you do here can break the website** — the dashboard only controls content, not the site itself
